Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 24, 2026

App: Shadow Strike

Developer: Dicky Hui

Overview

Shadow Strike ("the App") is a free mobile game. This policy explains what information is handled when you use the App. We designed the App to collect as little data as possible.

Information we collect

We do not collect, sell, or share personal information.

The App stores the following locally on your device only:

  • Whether you dismissed the tutorial
  • Whether you chose to skip story cutscenes

This data never leaves your device and is not transmitted to our servers. There is no user account, login, or cloud sync in version 1.0.

Information we do not collect

  • Name, email, or contact details
  • Location data
  • Contacts, photos, or files
  • Advertising identifiers for tracking
  • Analytics or crash reporting (v1.0)

Third-party services

Version 1.0 does not integrate third-party analytics, advertising, or social login. If this changes in a future update, this policy will be updated before release.

Google Play may collect information according to Google's Privacy Policy when you download or update the App through the Play Store.

Children

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data retention

Local preferences remain on your device until you uninstall the App or clear app data.

Your rights

Because we do not collect personal data on our servers, there is no remote profile to access or delete. Uninstalling the App removes locally stored preferences.

Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date at the top will change. Continued use after updates means you accept the revised policy.
