Storyline

When the Sundering split the surface into hex marches, the undercities did not float away — they fell, stacking into a vertical labyrinth called the Underrealm. The emperor's assassins were buried with their orders still ringing in the dark.

Clans broke their oaths and made new ones: kill inside the dungeons, truce on the surface. In Shadow Strike, you descend floor by floor as an assassin hunting corrupted wardens and void-touched beasts. Combo chains are your language; dodge timing is your religion.

Every fifth floor, a boss waits — not a monster, but a fragment-anchor holding shards apart. Break it, and somewhere above, a border shifts. The assassins know the final anchor must fall for the realm to heal. They intend to be the blade that falls last.

Frequently asked questions

What devices are supported?

iOS and Android via React Native and Expo. Run npm run phone from the project root to start the dev client.

What genre is Shadow Strike?

An action brawler dungeon crawler with combos, dodges, and periodic boss encounters.

Where does it fit in the lore?

The Underrealm beneath the hex marches — the same empire that Realm Conquest lords fight to reunite from above.

Is it free?

Yes. Optional REALM rewards via GameFi on Optimism for committed players.
