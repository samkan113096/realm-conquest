How to play

🕹️

Left stick — Evade

Move Kira around the arena. Enemies rush you — stay mobile or take damage up close.

🔫

Hold SHOOT — Auto-aim

Energy blasts lock onto the nearest foe. Kite and fire like a classic arcade shooter.

💥

BOMB — Clear swarms

Tap when surrounded. Blasts every enemy nearby. Cooldown — use it wisely.

🐍

Boss every 5 floors

Story cutscenes, syndicate bosses, and escalating difficulty. Skip story anytime.
