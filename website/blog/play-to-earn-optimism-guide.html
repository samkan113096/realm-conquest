REALM token, L2 speed, and how Realm GameFi links every standalone game game.

Why Optimism for GameFi

Ethereum L1 gas kills micro-rewards. Optimism's rollup posts batches to L1 while executing locally — perfect for daily claims, NFT mints, and stake deposits that should feel like loot, not paperwork. Realm contracts live on OP Stack infrastructure players already trust.

How REALM flows

Play any fragment → export session proof → submit on GameFi → receive REALM. Daily faucet adds baseline income for active wallets. Wallet setup tutorial walks connect, claim, mint, and stake step by step.

RHERO NFTs

RHERO heroes boost earn rates when staked. They are optional — all games stay free without NFTs. Mint costs testnet REALM today; mainnet TBA on the blog.

Risks and realism

Play to earn is not a salary. Token value fluctuates; testnet tokens have no market price. Treat REALM as a loyalty layer for fans who already love the games.

Get started

Setting up Optimism in your wallet

Add Optimism mainnet (chain ID 10) or Sepolia testnet (11155420) in MetaMask under Settings → Networks. Bridge a small ETH amount from Ethereum mainnet via the official Optimism bridge if you are on production; on testnet, use a public faucet for Sepolia ETH then bridge to OP Sepolia. You need ETH for gas even when rewards pay in REALM — claims are cheap on L2 but not gasless.

Never share seed phrases. Realm GameFi only requests connection signatures for claims you initiate. Phishing sites mimic connect buttons — bookmark the official GameFi page and verify the domain.

Daily claim loop explained

The daily faucet checks a lightweight play proof: any fragment session in the last twenty-four hours unlocks the button. Idle wallets earn nothing — design intent is to reward players, not farmers. Stack daily claims with milestone proofs from Realm Conquest victories, Neon Drift sector exports, or Shadow Strike floor clears for the best testnet accumulation.

Staking math (simplified)

Staking REALM deposits into a shared emission pool. Your share equals your stake divided by total stake, multiplied by weekly emissions. Attaching a staked RHERO applies a multiplier — roughly ten percent in current testnet parameters. Unstaking triggers cooldown so flash deposits cannot game weekly snapshots.

Tax and compliance notes

Token rewards may be taxable in your jurisdiction. Testnet REALM has no market value today; mainnet treatment will be announced with launch. Realm Games does not provide tax advice — consult professionals before treating play to earn as income.

Optimism's roadmap aligns with session-based rewards. If you play the portfolio anyway, connecting a wallet is the lowest-friction way to carry progress into the Chain Bridges lore fragment. Start with the GameFi tutorial and one daily claim habit.

Realm Conquest: export after victory screen. Neon Drift: export after sector 8 pause menu. Shadow Strike: sync from floor 10 pause. GameFi dashboard lists pending proofs — approve within seven days or they expire on testnet. Keep a personal spreadsheet of dates if you batch submissions weekly.
