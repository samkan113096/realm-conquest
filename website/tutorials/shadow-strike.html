Shadow Strike — Combat & Dungeon Guide

Descend the Underrealm: joystick movement, attack chains, specials, dodges, and every fifth-floor boss.

Shadow Strike is the mobile action brawler beneath the Hex Marches. Assassins descend the Underrealm with joystick combat, dodge frames, and combo finishers. Read Underrealm lore then follow this step-by-step guide.

Step 1 — Joystick movement

The left virtual stick controls eight-direction movement. Tap-to-move is disabled by default for precision. Slide your thumb along walls to kite melee brutes — collision does not slow you, but corners trap you against pack spawns.

Step 2 — Attack, special, and dodge

  • Attack (A) — light chain; third hit launches elites.
  • Special (B) — shadow burst, cooldown 8s; breaks shields.
  • Dodge (swipe) — i-frames; swipe toward threat, not away from walls.

Step 3 — Combo chains

Land five lights without taking damage to charge a finisher (hold attack). Finishers restore 15% HP on elites. Mix one special between chains to avoid cooldown waste on trash mobs.

Step 4 — Boss floors

Floors 5, 10, 15… spawn anchor guardians. Learn two phases: above 50% HP they summon adds; below 50% they gain area denial. Save special for phase two. Our Shadow Strike review breaks down each boss name and weakness.

Step 5 — Push deeper

Between floors, choose one relic: lifesteal, cooldown reduction, or combo window extension. Stack cooldown reduction with shadow burst for the fastest mobile brawler clear times. Link GameFi to mint progress proofs.
