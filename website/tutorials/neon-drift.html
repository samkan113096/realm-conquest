Neon Drift — Controls, Combos & 100-Wave Campaign

Fly Captain Nova Kane's Wraith Wing through the Orion Belt: chain combos, grab power-ups, fight dreadnought bosses, and save your run mid-campaign.

Neon Drift is a free PC arcade shooter set in the Orion Belt, 2187. You are Captain Nova Kane, flying the Wraith Wing through 100 warp gates while Viper Holdings sends drones and dreadnoughts to stop you. This guide matches the in-game tutorial and current build.

Step 1 — Controls

  • WASD or arrow keys — move in any direction; your ship turns toward your heading.
  • Space (hold) — fire in the direction you're facing.
  • ESC — pause and auto-save your campaign.
  • M — return to main menu (from pause or game over).
  • C — continue a saved campaign from the main menu.
  • T — open the in-game tutorial from the main menu.

Step 2 — Build combos

Each kill without taking hull damage adds to your combo multiplier — up to 20× score per kill. Taking any hit resets the chain to zero. Corporate drones (from Wave 2) grant higher combo weight than asteroids. Play aggressive when you have shield charges; play safe when your HP is low.

Step 3 — Power-ups

Pickups drop from destroyed enemies and asteroids during combat:

  • RAPID — fast fire for ~8 seconds
  • SPREAD — triple shot for ~10 seconds
  • NUKE — clears all enemies and asteroids; heavy boss damage
  • SHIELD — +2 shield charges that block hits
  • HEAL — +1 HP
  • SPEED — increased movement speed

SPREAD + RAPID overlapping is the best burst DPS for boss gates.

Step 4 — Boss gates & waves

The campaign runs 100 waves. Non-boss waves show a CLEAR X/Y kill quota on the HUD. Every 5th wave (5, 10, 15 … 100) is a Syndicate Dreadnought — defeat the boss to advance. Bosses move, fire at you, and display an HP bar at the top of the screen. Wave 5 starts with Ironjaw Tanaka; Wave 100 ends with The Board — Final Executive.

Step 5 — Save, pause & high scores

  • ESC pauses and auto-saves wave, score, HP, buffs, and boss state.
  • Continue Campaign on the main menu resumes a saved run — a full 100-wave clear can take hours across sessions.
  • Death clears your save but keeps your high score in savegame.json.
  • Clear Wave 100 for the Galaxy Freed victory screen.
  • Read the story panel on the main menu for Orion Belt lore before you launch.

Frequently asked questions

What platforms run Neon Drift?

PC via Python and Pygame on Windows, macOS, and Linux. Run npm run pc from the project root.

How many waves are in Neon Drift?

100 warp gates. Every 5th wave is a Syndicate Dreadnought boss fight.

Can I save my progress?

Yes. Pause with ESC to auto-save. Continue Campaign from the main menu to resume.

How do combos work?

Chain kills without taking hull damage to multiply score up to 20×. Any hit resets the combo.
