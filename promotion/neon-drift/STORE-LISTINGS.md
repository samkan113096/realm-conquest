## Full description (Steam / itch.io style)

```
The Orion Belt smuggler lanes don't forgive slow pilots.

NEON DRIFT is a top-down arcade space shooter — neon cyan and magenta on infinite dark, 60 FPS roguelike energy. You are a rogue pilot outrunning corporate drones through debris fields. Asteroids today. Armed pursuers tomorrow. One more run. One more record.

▸ GAMEPLAY
• WASD / arrow keys — move and rotate your ship
• Space — shoot
• Destroy asteroids for score; combo multiplier chains kills
• Enemies from wave 2 onward — escalating difficulty
• Boss dreadnought every 5 waves
• Power-ups: shield, speed, heal, rapid fire, spread shot
• Permadeath per run — high score saved locally
• 100-wave campaign with save & continue mid-run

▸ STORY
Captain Nova Kane. The Wraith Wing syndicate. The belt runs on blood and bandwidth — your ship is the only thing between you and corporate reclamation drones.

▸ FOR FANS OF
Classic arcade shooters, roguelikes, cyberpunk aesthetics, "one more run" games.

Launch: npm run pc  ·  Python 3 + Pygame
```
