Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 23, 2026

Chain Loot ("we", "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect when you use chainloot.io, our dApp, and community channels.

Information We Collect

  • Wallet addresses — when you connect a wallet, your public address is used for on-chain transactions. We do not store private keys.
  • Gameplay scores — submitted scores for play-to-earn claims are processed by our signer service and recorded on-chain only when you claim rewards.
  • Analytics — we may use privacy-friendly analytics (page views, referrers) to improve the site. No personal identifiers are sold.
  • Contact messages — if you email or message us on Telegram/Twitter, we retain correspondence to provide support.

Blockchain Data

Transactions on Optimism are public and permanent. Your wallet activity on Chain Loot contracts is visible on block explorers.

Cookies

We use minimal local storage for wallet connection preferences in the dApp. The marketing site does not use tracking cookies beyond essential functionality.

Third Parties

We use MetaMask and other wallet providers, Optimism RPC nodes, and hosting infrastructure. Each has its own privacy policy.

Your Rights

You may request deletion of off-chain support correspondence by emailing support@chainloot.io. On-chain data cannot be deleted.

Questions: support@chainloot.io · Contact page
