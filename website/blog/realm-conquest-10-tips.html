Realm Conquest — 10 Tips to Dominate the Hex Marches

Advanced economy, combat, and AI exploitation for lords who already know the basics.

May 16, 2026 · By Realm Games Team

You beat the tutorial. Now win campaigns. These ten Realm Conquest tips come from internal playtests and community Discord — use them in the browser build.

1. Third-turn farm rule

Build a farm by turn 3 unless you have a guaranteed neutral capture that includes fertile soil.

2. Starve before siege

Cut enemy farm clusters two turns before assaulting their capital. Army strength decays with food.

3. Archer bait

Soften border stacks with archers; warriors clean up. Never reverse the order on fortified hexes.

4. Choke scouting

Procedural maps hide one-tile bridges. Hold them with a single archer while you expand elsewhere.

5. Market timing

Markets after second barracks — gold spikes fund late warriors.

6. AI tells

Gold-heavy AI collapses when you capture their only farm. Food-heavy AI overextends — punish empty capitals.

7. Neutral gold hexes

Prioritize neutral gold over empty grass unless food is below three turns reserve.

8. Don't empty the capital

One unit minimum always. AI loves coward kills.

9. Endgame focus fire

When two rivals remain, eliminate the weaker entirely before touching the stronger.

10. Link GameFi last

Win first, connect wallet second — export victory proofs for bonus REALM. See master all games for portfolio earn.

Practice drills

Food panic drill: Win only by starving AI — teaches farm targeting. Speed expand drill: Win by turn twenty with no barracks until turn twelve. Archer defense drill: Hold a one-hex choke with three archers against warrior rush AI.

Common mistakes

Overbuilding markets before army exists. Splitting forces across three fronts. Attacking fortified hexes without archer soften. Ignoring the weakest rival while two remain.

Map seed literacy

Seeds with central lakes favor choke bridges. Seeds with scattered gold favor early markets. Screenshot your seed on victory and share in Discord #conquest-seeds.
