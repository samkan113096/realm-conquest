How to Play Chain Loot

Complete guide to Zombie Siege · Live on Ethereum Sepolia testnet

Chain Loot is independent from other games in this portfolio (Realm Conquest, Neon Drift, Shadow Strike). Everything you earn comes from Zombie Siege and on-chain LOOT mechanics inside the Chain Loot dApp.

1. Connect Your Wallet

Open the dApp and click Connect Wallet. MetaMask is recommended.

Testnet (live — Ethereum Sepolia)

  1. Open dApp on Netlify
  2. MetaMask → Sepolia network (chain ID 11155111)
  3. Connect wallet → earn LOOT via Play or community airdrop

Local development setup

  1. Terminal 1: cd gamefi && npx hardhat node
  2. Terminal 2: cd gamefi && npm run deploy:local
  3. Terminal 3: cd gamefi && npm run signer (site on :3001)
  4. Terminal 4: cd gamefi/dapp && npm run dev (dApp on :5173)

Add network to MetaMask:

  • Name: Hardhat Local
  • RPC: http://127.0.0.1:8545
  • Chain ID: 31337

Import Hardhat account #0 private key (public test key only):

0xac0974bec39a17e36ba4a6b4d238ff944bacb478cbed5efcae784d7bf4f2ff80

2. Get LOOT

There is no public faucet. Get LOOT by:

  • Play Zombie Siege — win fights to mint LOOT on-chain
  • Marketplace — buy LOOT-priced NFTs or trade with other players
  • Community airdrops — when we run a campaign, your wallet is registered on the LootAirdrop contract and you claim or receive LOOT

3. Mint a Hero NFT (50 LOOT)

Go to the Play or Heroes tab and click Mint Hero (50 LOOT).

Each hero is an ERC-721 (CLOOT) with on-chain randomized:

  • Class: Warrior, Ranger, Mage, Tank, or Assassin
  • Rarity: Common, Uncommon, Rare, Epic, or Legendary
  • Stats: Power, Speed, Defense, Luck

Max supply: 10,000 heroes globally. Max 20 per wallet.

4. Mint & Equip a Weapon (25 LOOT)

Mint a WLOOT weapon NFT for 25 LOOT. Weapons roll type (Sword/Bow/Staff/Hammer/Dagger), damage, crit, and rarity.

Select your hero and weapon in the Play tab, then click Equip to Hero. This calls equipWeapon() on the ZombieSiege contract on-chain.

Max supply: 20,000 weapons.

5. Fight Zombies in Zombie Siege

This is Chain Loot's play-to-earn game. All combat resolves on-chain — no external games, no score signer.

Hero HP (anti-grind)

Every hero has on-chain HP based on Defense, Power, class, and rarity. Each fight costs HP win or lose — you cannot grind forever on one hero.

  • HP regenerates passively: 10 HP per hour (on-chain)
  • Minimum 15 HP required to enter a fight
  • Higher zombie tiers cost more HP per battle
  • Tank and Legendary heroes have more max HP — mint better heroes or rotate your roster
  1. Open the 🧟 Play tab
  2. Check your Combat Power (hero stats + rarity + class + weapon)
  3. Pick a zombie tier you meet the minimum power for:
  • Shambler — 40 power · 2 LOOT · 12 HP cost
  • Runner — 70 power · 5 LOOT · 18 HP
  • Brute — 110 power · 10 LOOT · 26 HP
  • Elite — 160 power · 20 LOOT · 36 HP
  • Necromancer — 220 power · 40 LOOT · 48 HP

Click Fight On-Chain and confirm in MetaMask. The smart contract rolls win chance based on your power vs the zombie. Wins mint LOOT directly to your wallet. Each hero has a 30-second cooldown between fights and loses HP every battle.

Epic and Legendary heroes earn bonus LOOT on victories.

6. Stake LOOT (Optional)

On the Staking tab, approve and stake LOOT for passive rewards. Claim anytime without unstaking your principal.

7. Trade NFTs (Optional)

The Market tab links to the GameMarketplace contract where you can list CLOOT heroes and WLOOT weapons for LOOT.

Troubleshooting

  • Contracts not deployed — run npm run deploy:local with Hardhat node running
  • Wrong network — switch MetaMask to Sepolia (11155111) for live testnet, or Hardhat Local (31337) for dev
  • Hero too weak — mint a weapon, equip it, or mint another hero for better rarity
  • Hero resting — wait 30 seconds between fights (on-chain cooldown)
  • Hero HP too low — wait for passive regen (~10 HP/hr) or mint/use another hero
  • Gas limit too high / mint failed — usually wrong network or insufficient LOOT; fight zombies or wait for an airdrop
  • Future networks — Optimism mainnet planned after Sepolia testnet phase
