About Chain Loot

Standalone GameFi · Zombie Siege on Optimism

Chain Loot is an independent play-to-earn platform on Optimism. It is not connected to Realm Conquest, Neon Drift, Shadow Strike, or any other title — it is its own game ecosystem built around Zombie Siege.

What we built

  • LOOT Token — ERC-20 reward currency (1B max supply)
  • Hero NFTs (CLOOT) — 5 classes, 5 rarities, 10,000 max supply
  • Weapon NFTs (WLOOT) — equippable gear, 20,000 max supply
  • Zombie Siege — on-chain P2E zombie combat; fights and LOOT rewards via upgradeable ZombieSiege UUPS proxy
  • Staking — passive LOOT emissions
  • Marketplace — trade heroes and weapons for LOOT
  • Daily Faucet — 10 LOOT every 24 hours

Our mission

Make Web3 gaming transparent and fun — every fight, mint, and reward verifiable on-chain. No hidden score servers for the core game loop.
