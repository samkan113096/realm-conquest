NFT Heroes RHERO Explained — Stake, Boost & Trade

Everything about RHERO hero NFTs on Optimism and how they interact with REALM.

May 14, 2026 · By Realm Games Team

RHERO NFT heroes are the collectible face of Realm GameFi — minted on Optimism, staked for earn multipliers, and tied to Chronicle IV lore (Chain Bridges). Here is what they do and what they do not do.

What RHERO is

Each RHERO is a unique hero portrait with trait tiers (Common, Rare, Ascendant). Traits flavor lore; stake weight comes from rarity. Mint requires REALM (500 on testnet) via the GameFi tutorial.

Stake mechanics

Stake RHERO + REALM in the vault to increase your share of weekly emission pools. Unstaking cooldown prevents flash farming. Heroes are not required to play any game.

What RHERO is not

Not pay-to-win units in Realm Conquest or Shadow Strike. Not a promise of profit. Not transferable to mainnet until official bridge announcement.

Trading and metadata

Testnet trades use open market UI on GameFi. Metadata points to standalone game hero names — assassins, pilots, lords — unified for collectors building sets.

Earn path without NFT

Daily claims and run proofs still work wallet-only. RHERO is +10% for stakers, not a gate. Read play to earn guide.

Mint your hero

Open GameFi when you have enough REALM.

Trait tables and rarity

Common RHERO portraits drop standard stake weight. Rare variants add cosmetic auras in GameFi profiles. Ascendant tiers carry higher stake multipliers and unique lore voice lines. Traits do not alter combat in Shadow Strike or units in Realm Conquest.

Minting walkthrough recap

Accumulate REALM → open GameFi mint panel → confirm Optimism transaction → stake from vault. Screenshots in the GameFi tutorial. Failed transactions usually mean insufficient ETH for gas.

Secondary market caution

Testnet trades are practice only. Scammers DM fake mint links. Verify contract addresses on official channels.

Collecting sets

Heroes group into assassin, pilot, and lord lineages. Future lore events may reference lineages; nothing announced yet.

RHERO is optional for earn optimizers and collectors. Open GameFi when your REALM stack is ready.

Vault security

Use hardware wallets for high-value mainnet mints when launch arrives. Testnet can stay hot wallet. Revoke old token approvals quarterly via Etherscan approval tools on Optimism.

Lore tie-ins

Each RHERO name appears in Chronicle IV footnotes — collectors hunt easter eggs in lore updates. Assassin-line heroes reference Shadow Strike bosses; lord-line heroes reference Conquest houses.
