Realm GameFi — Wallet to Rewards

From wallet connect to daily claims, NFT heroes, staking, and cross-game play-to-earn on Optimism.

Realm GameFi connects Realm Conquest, Neon Drift, and Shadow Strike through REALM token on Optimism. This tutorial walks through wallet setup, daily claims, NFT minting, staking, and play-to-earn.

Step 1 — Wallet setup

  1. Install MetaMask, Rabby, or Coinbase Wallet.
  2. Add the Optimism network (chain ID 10 mainnet or 11155420 Sepolia testnet).
  3. Open the GameFi hub and click Connect Wallet.
  4. Sign the read-only connection prompt — no gas yet.

Step 2 — Claim daily REALM

Once per UTC day, tap Claim Daily. You must have played any fragment in the last 24 hours (verified by a lightweight session proof). Testnet faucet refreshes every 24h — see play to earn on Optimism for economics.

Step 3 — Mint RHERO NFT

Accumulate 500 REALM (testnet) to mint a RHERO hero NFT. Heroes grant +10% earn rate when staked. Minting is one transaction on Optimism — confirm gas in your wallet. Details in RHERO explained.

Step 4 — Stake REALM

Stake unlocked REALM in the vault for emission share of weekly pools. Unstaking has a 48-hour cooldown on testnet. Staked heroes (RHERO) multiply your share — read web3 gaming 2026 for why L2 staking matters.

Step 5 — Play-to-earn claims

After milestones (conquest victory, Neon Drift sector 8+, Shadow Strike floor 10), export proof from each game and submit on GameFi. Approved claims credit REALM within one Optimism block confirmation. Stack with daily claims for maximum portfolio earnings.

Frequently asked questions

Which network does Realm GameFi use?

Optimism L2 — fast confirmations and low fees for daily claims and staking.

Do I need ETH on Optimism?

A small amount of ETH on Optimism pays gas; REALM is the reward and stake token.

Are RHERO NFTs required to play?

No. Heroes boost earn rates; all four games are free without NFTs.

Is this mainnet or testnet?

Testnet faucet and claims are live; mainnet launch is announced on the blog.
