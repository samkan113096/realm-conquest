Realm Conquest — Complete How to Play Guide

Master the free hex strategy game: from your first farm to the final siege of rival lords in the Hex Marches.

This complete how-to-play guide for Realm Conquest covers economy, buildings, units, combat, and victory conditions in four independent game worlds. No install required — open the browser build and follow each step.

Step 1 — Know your resources

Every turn you manage three pillars: Gold (markets and trade), Food (farms and population), and Army strength (barracks and recruits). Gold buys buildings and units; food sustains your army; army strength wins border fights. Neglect any leg and rival AI lords in the Hex Marches will punish you.

Step 2 — Expand on the hex map

Click adjacent neutral or enemy hexes to move and claim territory. Early expansion secures resources; over-expansion spreads food thin. Capture clusters with farm bonuses before chasing distant borders.

Step 3 — Build farms, barracks, and markets

  1. Farms — place on fertile tiles; they raise food per turn.
  2. Barracks — unlock warrior and archer recruitment.
  3. Markets — boost gold income for late-game unit spam.

Build order tip: one farm before your second barracks unless you are rushing a weak neighbor.

Step 4 — Recruit and position units

Warriors excel at front-line pushes; archers soften enemies before melee engages. Stack units on border hexes facing the strongest AI lord. Never leave your capital hex empty late game.

Step 5 — Combat resolution

When you attack an occupied hex, the game compares combined army strength plus unit bonuses. Defenders on fortified tiles get implicit advantage — probe with archers, then commit warriors. If you lose, you lose units and momentum; pause expansion and rebuild food.

Step 6 — Win the campaign

Victory requires eliminating all rival AI lords or controlling the victory percentage shown in the endgame panel (varies by map seed). Starve the last lord by cutting their farm clusters before the final assault.

  • Scout AI build patterns — some lords prioritize gold and collapse when food-blocked.

Most browser sessions run 20–45 minutes depending on map size and AI difficulty.

What happens if I run out of food?
