Storyline

When the Sundering broke the Eternal Empire, surface land crystallized into perfect hex tiles — neutral ground between hungry houses. You awaken in a keep with one village, a handful of spears, and a claim older than the fracture itself.

Other lords circle the same map. They build farms for food, markets for gold, barracks for warriors and archers. You must decide each turn: expand fast and starve, or fortify and be overrun. Procedural maps mean no two campaigns share the same war — only the same desperate hope that your fragment becomes the spine of a reunited realm.

Deep turn-based strategy without installs or paywalls.
