Chain Loot is a standalone play-to-earn platform on Ethereum Sepolia testnet. Mint hero & weapon NFTs, equip them, and battle zombies in Zombie Siege — every fight and reward settles on-chain.

Play Zombie Siege Full Tutorial

🧟 Zombie Siege — Our Play-to-Earn Game

Built exclusively for Chain Loot. No external games, no score oracles — combat resolves in the upgradeable ZombieSiege UUPS proxy. Defeat zombies → earn LOOT on-chain.

On-Chain Combat

Call fight(heroId, tier) from the dApp. Win chance scales with your hero's combat power vs zombie tier. Victories mint LOOT instantly.

5 Zombie Tiers

  • Shambler — 40 power · 2 LOOT
  • Runner — 70 power · 5 LOOT
  • Brute — 110 power · 10 LOOT
  • Elite — 160 power · 20 LOOT
  • Necromancer — 220 power · 40 LOOT

Hero + Weapon Power

Combat power = hero stats + rarity bonus + class bonus + equipped weapon damage. Epic & Legendary heroes earn bonus LOOT on wins.

Hero NFTs (CLOOT)

Up to 10,000 unique heroes. Each mint rolls class, rarity, and stats on-chain.

5 Classes

Warrior · Ranger · Mage · Tank · Assassin — each adds a combat bonus in Zombie Siege.

5 Rarities

Common → Uncommon → Rare → Epic → Legendary. Higher rarity = better stats & LOOT multipliers.

On-Chain Stats

Power, Speed, Defense, Luck — rolled at mint. Visible in token URI metadata.

Mint Cost

50 LOOT per hero. Max 20 heroes per wallet.

Weapon NFTs (WLOOT)

Up to 20,000 weapons. Equip to heroes before fighting zombies.

5 Weapon Types

Sword · Bow · Staff · Hammer · Dagger

Damage & Crit

Each weapon rolls damage and crit stats based on rarity.

Equip On-Chain

equipWeapon(heroId, weaponId) in ZombieSiege contract before each battle.

Mint Cost

25 LOOT per weapon.

More Ways to Earn

Community Airdrops

LOOT distributed via admin LootAirdrop contract — campaign allocations for outreach, not a public faucet.

Stake LOOT

Deposit in the staking vault and earn passive emissions over time.

NFT Marketplace

Trade CLOOT heroes and WLOOT weapons peer-to-peer for LOOT.

Sepolia Testnet

Live on Ethereum Sepolia (chain 11155111). Low gas for minting, fighting, and marketplace trades.
