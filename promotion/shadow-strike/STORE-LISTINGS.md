## Full description (Google Play — up to 4,000 characters)

```
Neo-Tokyo, 2187. The Underground Circuit has no rules, no mercy — only glory and blood.

You are KIRA ASHIDA, codename SHADOW STRIKE. Once a corporate security operative, you walked away after uncovering syndicate fight-rigging. Now you fight your way up the illegal pits to expose Viper Holdings — one floor at a time.

SHADOW STRIKE is a fast-paced top-down arena shooter built for mobile. Enemies rush you. You shoot back. Stay moving or die.

▸ HOW TO PLAY
• LEFT THUMB — Move Kira to evade chasing foes
• HOLD SHOOT — Energy blasts auto-aim at the nearest enemy
• TAP BOMB — Clear nearby enemies when you're swarmed (cooldown)

▸ FEATURES
• Endless floor-based runs — clear every enemy to advance
• Boss fights every 5 floors with story cutscenes
• Auto-aim shooting — pick off targets while you kite
• Kill streak multiplier for chained takedowns
• Skip or disable story dialogue anytime
• Built-in tutorial for first-time players
• Landscape controls optimized for thumbs

▸ THE STORY
Rise through Neo-Tokyo's illegal fight circuit. Face pit enforcers, syndicate champions, and the directors of Viper Holdings. Every floor brings heavier hitters — and louder crowds.

▸ PERFECT FOR
Fans of arcade shooters, arena survival, and sci-fi action. Easy to pick up, hard to put down.

No account required. No paywalls. Just you, the pit, and the syndicate.

Download Shadow Strike. Enter the circuit.
```
