## Short description (store / Discord / Reddit)

**Neon Drift** — neon arcade shooter set in the Orion Belt smuggler lanes, 2187.

You are **Captain Nova Kane**, last free ace in the sector. Viper Holdings locked every jump gate and sent drone fleets to hunt survivors. Pilot the **Wraith Wing** through **100 warp gates**, each wave deeper into hostile territory.

- **8-way movement** — WASD or arrow keys; ship turns toward your heading  
- **Combo scoring** — chain kills without taking damage  
- **Power-ups** — RAPID, SPREAD, NUKE, SHIELD, HEAL, SPEED  
- **Boss every 5 waves** — named Syndicate Dreadnoughts with unique HP bars  
- **Save anytime** — pause or quit; **Continue Campaign** on the main menu  
- **Galaxy backdrop** — nebula parallax starfield  

Free · PC · Pygame · `npm run pc` from repo root.
