    How to Play

    1. Solo — Play vs Computer: fight AI lords (Red Legion & Shadow Court). No login.
    2. Online — Google sign-in only. Each turn is limited to 3 minutes; if time runs out, your opponent plays.
    3. Select your house's territory on the hex map.
    4. Build Farm → Barracks → Market, recruit warriors & archers.
    5. Move / Attack — click the action, then pick a highlighted hex (map or sidebar list).
    6. Attack up to 3 times per turn on adjacent enemies.
    7. Win by holding 70% of Aldoria.
